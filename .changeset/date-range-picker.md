Add `DateRangePicker` — trigger + popover calendar for picking a start/end date range, with quick-select presets (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month, Last month), `min`/`max` bounds, and `density` support. No new runtime dependency: built on native `Date` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat`, no date library added.

A single-date/time-of-day picker is out of scope for this change.
